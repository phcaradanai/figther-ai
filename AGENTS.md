# Repository workflow

After completing and verifying a change in this repository, commit it and merge it into `main` before reporting the work complete.

Keep WASD for movement, J for jump/glide/wake-up, K for guard/dodge, L for basic/dash/jump attacks and hold-L charge/ranged aiming, and KL/JKL/JL/JK (with Q/E/R/F shortcuts) for weapon/armor/helmet/trinket gear skills.
